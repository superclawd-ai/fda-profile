# Tickets

## Writing one

- Search for duplicates first; link instead of filing twice.
- **Title**: the outcome and the area ("Checkout: card errors show a blank page"), not "Bug".
- **Body**: the problem in user terms; steps to reproduce or context; expected vs actual; acceptance
  criteria as a checklist; links (thread, screenshot, logs); a suggested priority with the reason.
- Keep personal and customer data out unless it's needed to reproduce, and then the minimum.

## Triage

Group by theme, merge duplicates (link them, don't delete), and flag items with no owner, no acceptance
criteria, or no activity in a long time. Propose, don't silently change priorities or owners unless the
team asked you to.

## Status report

Cover a stated window ("since Monday"). Order: shipped → in progress → blocked (and on whom) → at risk.
Put counts in a small table and link each item. Lead with the one thing people most need to know.
