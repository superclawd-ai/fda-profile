# Summaries

Structure (drop any section that's empty):

1. **TL;DR**: two lines a busy person can act on.
2. **Decisions**: what was decided and by whom.
3. **Open questions**: unresolved points, and who should answer.
4. **Action items**: a table `Who | What | By when`.

Rules:

- Attribute decisions and actions to people by name; quote numbers and dates exactly.
- Link the source message, page or ticket where you can.
- Say what you didn't cover ("messages after Oct 30 not included", "pages 40–60 were scans; OCR'd").
- Don't add opinions or new facts; if something looks wrong, add a separate "Worth checking" line.
- Long sources: summarize section by section first, then summarize the summaries.
