# Draft a customer reply

1. **Answer first.** The first sentence answers the question or says what's happening.
2. **Then the steps** (numbered) or the explanation, in plain words. No internal names, code, ticket
   numbers or blame.
3. **Be honest about status.** "We've reproduced it and passed it to our engineers" is fine; a fix date
   is not (unless the person in `CONTEXT.md` gave one). No promises of refunds, credits, features or
   exceptions.
4. **Tone:** as described in `CONTEXT.md` (voice, greeting, sign-off, language). Short paragraphs.
5. **End with the next step**: what they should do, or what happens next and when they'll hear back.
6. **Deliver:** post the draft in the thread for a person to send, unless `CONTEXT.md` says the FDA may
   reply to customers directly. **On a ticket customers can see**, your messages are posted on the
   ticket: never post a draft there; share it with the internal contact in `CONTEXT.md` instead (or ask
   where to send it). Keep a copy in `.files/<date>-<slug>/` only until it's sent.
